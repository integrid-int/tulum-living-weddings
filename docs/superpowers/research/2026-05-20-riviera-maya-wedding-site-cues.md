Date: 2026-05-20

## Common design patterns observed

### 1) High-impact hero with emotional promise
- Every site opens with full-bleed destination imagery.
- Headline formula: premium service promise + location context.
- Primary CTA appears above the fold ("Book consultation", "Start planning", "Let's chat").

### 2) Social proof as a primary visual block
- Repeating patterns:
  - years in market,
  - count of events,
  - ratings/reviews badges,
  - "featured in" trust markers.
- These are surfaced before deep service detail.

### 3) Process storytelling in 3-4 clear steps
- Successful sites simplify planning complexity with sequence blocks:
  - discovery,
  - design,
  - coordination,
  - wedding day execution.
- This reduces anxiety and increases conversion confidence.

### 4) Service cards with stronger hierarchy
- Service offerings are segmented into focused cards/tiles.
- Each card emphasizes outcome, not only task list.
- Card-driven layouts create scannability on mobile and desktop.

### 5) Destination-forward brand texture
- Frequent mention of destination clusters (Tulum, Riviera Maya, Cancun, Playa del Carmen).
- Warm neutral palettes + premium serif headings are common.
- Sites lean into editorial/luxury cues more than "app-like" UI.

## Adopted cues for this refresh

- Added a premium trust-signals section on home with hard proof metrics.
- Added a four-step planning journey section to communicate process clarity.
- Enhanced section composition with stronger container rhythm, depth, and card styling.
- Upgraded typography with serif-forward heading treatment and refined body pairing.
- Improved navigation experience with sticky header and high-visibility consultation CTA.

## Notes

- The refresh intentionally keeps the existing content model and CMS wiring intact.
- This pass focuses on visual hierarchy, perceived trust, and conversion-oriented UX.
